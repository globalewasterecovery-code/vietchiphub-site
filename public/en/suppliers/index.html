What buyers should provide

Manufacturer, exact part number, package, grade, quantity, acceptable date code, required documents, delivery city and target date.

Technical and compatibility information is for sourcing support only. Buyers remain responsible for engineering validation, inspection and final approval.

Need a fast review?

Common questions

Does the platform guarantee authenticity?

No. We organize supplier evidence and recommend appropriate inspection; final acceptance belongs to the buyer.

Can alternative parts be proposed?

Yes, but alternatives are clearly marked and require engineering and buyer approval.

Can suppliers submit stock?

Yes. Inventory owners can use the supplier route and provide labels, quantities, condition and location.

Supplier registration

Introduce your business and the inventory you own or are authorized to offer. We review your details privately before any public listing.

Inventory & authorization

Registration does not create verified supplier status or publish inventory. Authorization, ownership and available quantity are reviewed separately.
